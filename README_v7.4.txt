BF1GameExt v7.4 (build p25j-r1) - v7.3 (p25i-r1) + the 1-player side carousel made optional, Oct 28 2026.

- MAIN MENU > OPTIONS > MODS: a new row SIDE CAROUSEL (ON / OFF), saved at once in BF1GameExt.ini as
      [ClassWheel]
      SideCarousel=1        ; 1 = carousel (default), 0 = rows
  ON: single player's CHOOSE YOUR SIDE with more than two sides is the carousel (v7.1 - v7.3), defeated sides left off.
  OFF: single player gets v7.0's rows instead (3 / 4 in a row, 5 = 3+2, 6 = 3+3, 7 = 4+3), defeated sides greyed out.
  Split screen (2 - 4 players) always uses the carousel (the rows do not fit a half / quarter).
- Log: [wheel] "[ClassWheel] SideCarousel=N", "MODS page SIDE CAROUSEL: ON / OFF".
- The build string: p25j-r1.
