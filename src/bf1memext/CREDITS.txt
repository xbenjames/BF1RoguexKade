The extended limits - src\bf1memext

limits.cpp            BF1MemExt's limit patches ([Limits] Heaps, HiResUnits, MatrixPool, DLCMissions), brought into
                      BF1GameExt so they work on GOG 1.3 and Steam (BF1MemExt itself only supports GOG 1.2),
                      plus BF1GameExt's own addition: [Limits] MainHeapMB.
bf1memext_loader.cpp  on GOG 1.2 only: loads BF1MemExt.dll itself at the game's start, without patching the exe.
