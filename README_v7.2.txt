BF1GameExt v7.2 (build p25h-r1) - v7.1 (p25g-r1) + Ben's two changes, Oct 27 2026.

- The side carousel (a match with more than two sides) no longer shows the sides that are out of the match: a defeated
  side is taken off the carousel (v7.1 greyed it out). If one goes out while the screen is up, the carousel is made again
  without it and the side you had picked stays picked. A match with exactly two sides still greys an out side, as before.
- "SELECT SIDE" at the bottom right of CHOOSE YOUR SIDE (across from BACK, like SELECT SPAWNPOINT / SELECT CHARACTER on
  the next screens), for player 1 and player 2. Clicking it joins the picked side.
- Log: [wheel] "a side is out of the match - the carousel made again without it", "click on SELECT SIDE".
